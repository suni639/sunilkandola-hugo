## 3. STRUCTURAL & OPERATIONAL PAIN POINTS
*   **Interoperability Silos:** The varied objections from major corporations to tokenised stock trading under the [SEC's](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/10/18/sec-tokenized-stock-framework-faces-issuer-pushback-as-broadcom-verizon-object-to-okx-ice-listing/) proposed framework, particularly on platforms like [OKX-ICE](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/10/18/sec-tokenized-stock-framework-faces-issuer-pushback-as-broadcom-verizon-object-to-okx-ice-listing/), underscore a fundamental lack of universally accepted standards, legal clarity, and seamless technical bridges for integrating tokenised securities across disparate trading venues and existing capital market infrastructure. This fragmentation risks creating isolated liquidity pools for digital assets. The formation of the [Quantum Alliance](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/10/16/experts-agree-quantum-computers-could-put-satoshi-s-bitcoin-at-risk-but-not-on-what-s-next/) also implicitly addresses the need for common, interoperable quantum-resistant solutions across existing digital asset networks.
*   **Balance Sheet & Liquidity Friction:** The [BIS's](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/10/14/banks-could-slow-stablecoin-growth-by-paying-savers-more-bis-paper-finds/) observation that commercial banks can slow stablecoin growth by offering higher deposit rates directly illustrates the competitive friction for institutional liquidity. Stablecoins present a direct alternative for efficient capital deployment, potentially disintermediating traditional bank deposits and forcing commercial banks to re-evaluate their funding costs, capital ratios, and balance sheet management strategies in a 24/7 digital money environment. [Circle's](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/10/17/circle-defends-usdc-economics-as-stablecoin-rivals-court-its-partners-with-bigger-cut/) defence of its [USDC](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/10/17/circle-defends-usdc-economics-as-stablecoin-rivals-court-its-partners-with-bigger-cut/) economic model against rivals offering larger revenue shares to partners further highlights the intense competition for institutional balance sheet adoption and associated liquidity.
*   **Post-Trade Plumbing Constraints:** The emergence and funding of platforms like [Multiliquid](https://www.theblock.co/news/deals/2026-10-15-multiliquid-tokenized-assets-funding-418693/) for atomic settlement of tokenised assets, alongside [Ripple Prime's](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026/10/15-ripple-prime-rwa-swaps-perpetual-futures-hyperliquid-418681/) introduction of RWA swaps with 24/7 trading and settlement capabilities, directly addresses the persistent post-trade bottlenecks in traditional finance. These innovations aim to overcome limitations such as batch processing, manual reconciliation, and restrictive operating hours, moving towards real-time, atomic delivery-versus-payment (DvP) and payment-versus-payment (PvP) settlement, thereby drastically improving capital efficiency and reducing counterparty risk within market infrastructure.
