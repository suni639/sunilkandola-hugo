## 1. MACRO VIEW
* **Commercial Banks Interlink Deposit Platforms to Support Continuous Dollar Transfers.** DBS and J.P. Morgan connected their proprietary platforms to process dollar payments between Singapore and the US at weekends. Corporate treasuries can move cash outside clearing hours without holding idle balances in foreign accounts.
* **Project Meridian and Agorá Prove Wholesale Foreign Exchange Atomic Settlement.** The Bank of England, Monetary Authority of Singapore and Bank for International Settlements completed tests settling cross-border trades in seconds. Synchronised central bank money eliminates credit risk during cross-currency transactions.
* **US Authorities and European Bodies Draft Rules for Tokenised Assets.** The Office of the Comptroller of the Currency set a January 2027 compliance deadline for bank stablecoins. European regulators launched a working group to create common settlement rules across member states.
* **Financial Infrastructure Operators Tokenise Equity Indices and Sovereign Securities.** Deutsche Börse affiliate 360X listed DAX constituents while Japan Securities Clearing Corporation tested Japanese Government Bond transfers. Securities settlement moves directly to distributed ledgers without traditional central depository delays.

Financial institutions can service tokenised assets across public and private networks without altering core balance sheet controls.

Incoming stablecoins automatically convert into cash deposits in client accounts.
* [Goldman, Citi, UBS, 18 others fund Project Faro stablecoin. * [BlackRock BUIDL to enable weekend USDC exchanges with up to $100 million liquidity](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/125604/blackrock-buidl-to-enable-weekend-usdc-exchanges-with-up-to-100-million-liquidity) *(디지털투데이)*: Securitise and Ethena launched a facility letting investors in BlackRock's BUIDL fund exchange tokens for USDC on weekends. Central banks completed cross-border foreign exchange synchronisation trials and demonstrated instant wholesale settlement. Financial market infrastructures are expanding wholesale digital currency acceptance into after-hours trading.

* [Bank of England, MAS, Bank of Thailand complete DLT synchronization trials](https://www.ledgerinsights.com/bank-of-england-mas-bank-of-thailand-complete-dlt-synchronization-trials/) *(Ledger Insights)*: The Bank of England, Monetary Authority of Singapore and Bank of Thailand completed Project Meridian FX APAC. * [HKEX to accept CBDC payments for derivatives after-hours trading](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48760/hkex-to-accept-cbdc-payments-for-derivatives-after-hours-trading) *(Finextra Research)*: Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing agreed to accept central bank digital currency payments for derivatives after-hours trading.

The trial links the Swift ledger to Project Ensemble's settlement layer.
* [BIS Project Agorá Settles Cross-Border Payments in Seconds: Report](https://coinmarketcap.com/academy/article/bis-project-agora-cross-border-payments) *(CoinMarketCap)*: The Bank for International Settlements completed a prototype for Project Agorá that settled cross-border payments in seconds. Seven central banks and over 40 financial institutions participated using tokenised reserves and bank deposits.
* [Bank of Spain's Escrivá Sets Out Europe's Tokenized Markets Plan as Pontes Connects 18 Institutions and Appia Targets 2028](https://genfinity.io/2026/11/11/bank-of-spain-escriva-pontes-appia-tokenized-markets-europe/) *(genfinity.io)*: Bank of Spain Governor José Luis Escrivá outlined European plans for tokenised markets using the Eurosystem's Pontes settlement platform. European authorities target 2028 for full integration under the Appia initiative.

### Regulatory & Legal Frameworks
Regulators issued binding compliance rules for bank stablecoin issuance and established industry working groups to standardise securities tokenisation.

* [Stablecoin Regulation: OCC Final Rule Takes Effect in 2027](https://www.onesafe.io/blog/stablecoin-regulation-occ-rule-web3-businesses) *(OneSafe)*: The Office of the Comptroller of the Currency set an 18 January 2027 compliance deadline for US national banks issuing or custodying payment stablecoins. Banks must establish capital buffers and supervisory agreements.
* [Treasury Proposal Would Add Due Diligence Rules for Stablecoin Issuers](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/regulation/57471) *(TOKENPOST)*: The US Treasury proposed rules requiring payment stablecoin issuers to conduct due diligence on third-party counterparties. The standards align counterparty checks with US correspondent banking rules.
* [ECB, European Commission, and ESMA Launch the European Tokenisation Standards Group to Build One Rulebook for Tokenized Securities](https://genfinity.io/2026/11/09/european-tokenisation-standards-group-ecb-esma-tokenized-securities/) *(genfinity.io)*: The European Central Bank, European Commission and ESMA established the European Tokenisation Standards Group on 9 November 2026. Industry experts will draft rules for holding and settling tokenised securities across blockchains.
* [Florida Becomes First U.S. State To Pass a Stablecoin Regulatory Bill](https://coinmarketcap.com/academy/article/florida-becomes-first-us-state-to-pass-a-stablecoin-regulatory-bill) *(CoinMarketCap)*: Florida passed Senate Bill 314 to regulate stablecoin issuers under its Office of Financial Regulation. The law classifies stablecoins as monetary value and allows state agencies to accept approved stablecoins for tax payments.

The system assists central bank researchers in specifying structural vector autoregression models.

## 3. STRUCTURAL & OPERATIONAL PAIN POINTS
* **Interoperability Silos:** Institutions operating separate private ledgers face fragmented liquidity pools across networks. Projects such as Prividium Connect and Swift's joint compliance initiative attempt to link incompatible ledgers, but institutions still lack unified settlement standards across public and private blockchains.
* **Balance Sheet & Liquidity Friction:** Operating 24/7 payment channels requires continuous liquidity management outside regular central bank operating hours. Services like the BlackRock BUIDL USDC liquidity facility provide weekend redemptions, but banks must retain dedicated reserves to cover off-hours fiat conversions.
* **Post-Trade Plumbing Constraints:** Legacy clearing systems struggle to process real-time securities transfers alongside batch-based clearing cycles. While the Bank of England and JSCC are testing DLT collateral management, market participants must maintain dual operational workflows until central depositories fully integrate DLT networks.
