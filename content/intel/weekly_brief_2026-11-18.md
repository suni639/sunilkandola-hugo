## In one minute
* **JPMorgan and DBS launch 24/7 tokenised deposit transfers.** DBS Bank and JPMorgan's Kinexys unit launched this service on 16 November for instant US dollar movements between Singapore and the US.
* **DTCC’s tokenisation service goes live on Canton Network.** The Depository Trust Company launched its tokenisation service on 28 October 2026, making $30 trillion in eligible assets available.
* **HKMA, Swift, HSBC, StanChart explore tokenised deposit payments.** The Swift Ledger launched earlier this year, with seven pilot banks already completing live transactions.

## The UK angle
* Monument Bank launched the UK’s first interest-bearing tokenised deposits on the Midnight Network.
* The Bank of England is consulting on its approach to central counterparties accepting tokenised collateral.
* UK-based HSBC and Standard Chartered are participating in the HKMA and Swift trial for cross-border tokenised deposit payments.

## What happened

### UK
* [Monument Bank launches UK tokenised deposits](https://midnight.network/blog/monument-bank-launches-the-uk-s-first-interest-bearing-tokenized-deposits-on-midnight) *(Midnight Network)*: Monument Bank launched the UK's first interest-bearing tokenised deposits on the Midnight Network.
* [Bank of England approach for tokenised collateral](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2026/11/11/tokenised-collateral-bank-of-england-sets-out-approach-for-central-clearing-102o99x) *(Lewis Silkin)*: The Bank of England is consulting on its approach to central counterparties (CCPs) accepting tokenised collateral. The proposals aim to give CCPs and participants greater clarity.

### Banks and payments
* [DBS, JPMorgan launch 24/7 tokenised deposit transfers](https://finance.biggo.com/news/75b92d67-f36f-42c4-a881-b8dcb356c271) *(BigGo Finance)*: DBS Bank and JPMorgan's Kinexys unit launched a commercial international remittance service on 16 November using tokenised deposits. Institutional clients can move US dollars instantly between accounts in the US and Singapore, including on weekends. Ant International is the first corporate user.
* [HKMA, Swift, banks explore tokenised deposit payments](https://www.ledgerinsights.com/hkma-hsbc-stanchart-explore-tokenized-deposit-payments-via-swift-ledger-and-ensemble/) *(Ledger Insights)*: The HKMA partnered with Swift to explore connecting Project Ensemble's settlement layer to the Swift Ledger. HSBC, Standard Chartered, and Bank of China (Hong Kong) are participating. The focus is on cross-border tokenised deposit payments in multiple currencies.

### Funds, securities and market infrastructure
* [DTCC tokenisation service live on Canton Network](https://www.coingabbar.com/en/dtcc-tokenization-service-30-trillion-assets-canton-network) *(Coin Gabbar)*: DTCC's tokenisation service went live on the Canton Network on 28 October 2026. The service makes $30 trillion in eligible assets available on-chain, covering US Treasuries, equities, and ETFs.
* [Talos, Marex, Broadridge for tokenised Treasury funding](https://www.ledgerinsights.com/talos-marex-broadridge-partner-for-dtcc-tokenized-treasury-funding-after-hours/) *(Ledger Insights)*: Talos, Marex, and Broadridge announced a collaboration regarding DTCC tokenised securities. Marex plans to offer prime brokerage financing linked to tokenised Treasuries outside market hours. Talos provides trade execution infrastructure and Broadridge provides post-trade infrastructure.
* [HKEX to accept wCBDC for derivatives margin](https://www.ledgerinsights.com/hkex-clearing-house-to-accept-wcbdc-for-derivatives-margin-from-december/) *(Ledger Insights)*: The HKEX clearing house will accept wholesale CBDC for derivatives margin from 14 December. Bank of China (Hong Kong), HSBC, and Standard Chartered are the three ready settlement banks.

### Central banks
* [Bundesbank, MAS test cross-border FX with wCBDC](https://www.ledgerinsights.com/bundesbank-mas-test-cross-border-fx-settlement-using-wholesale-cbdcs/) *(Ledger Insights)*: The Bundesbank and MAS published key takeaways from Project Simbolon, a proof of concept for FX payment-versus-payment (PvP) settlement. Deutsche Bank and DBS participated in the trial, exchanging EUR and SGD wholesale CBDCs.
* [ECB urges digital euro law, Pontes settles bond](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/11/11/ecb-urges-year-end-digital-euro-law-as-pontes-settles-first-1b-tokenized-bond/) *(The Crypto Times)*: The ECB urged EU lawmakers to complete negotiations on the digital euro's legal framework by the end of 2026. The Eurosystem's Pontes platform settled the European Stability Mechanism's first €1 billion digitally native bond on 2 November.

### Regulation and law
* [IOSCO report on financial asset tokenisation](https://www.iosco.org/news/pdf/IOSCONEWS809.pdf): IOSCO published its 2026 Monitoring Report on the Tokenisation of Financial Assets.

### New technology
* [Hyundai Card, JPMorgan test AI FX, remittance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/010fe306-b4fa-46e3-a3a8-738d6f39c93f) *(BigGo Finance)*: Hyundai Card and JPMorgan's Kinexys unit completed a proof of concept for automated FX and remittance between AI agents. AI agents exchanged information directly to handle foreign exchange and remittances using JPMorgan's Kinexys blockchain.
* [HKMA explores AI for crypto crime detection](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-11-18-hong-kong-agentic-ai-crypto-crime-442959) *(The Block)*: The HKMA is exploring agentic AI to detect crypto-related financial crime. AI could help analyse on-chain transfers and off-chain customer transactions to flag anomalies.
