## In one minute
* **UK Treasury names banks for first digitally native gilt.** A pilot for the gilt is expected by the first quarter of 2027.
* **UK Treasury amends stablecoin rules.** The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Cryptoassets) (Miscellaneous Amendments) Regulations 2026 came into force on 18 November.
* **IOSCO publishes final report on asset tokenisation.** IOSCO observed that tokenisation has progressed but remains transitional and fragmented.

## The UK angle
* HSBC and Standard Chartered were among the primary dealers for the Monetary Authority of Singapore's S$100 million tokenised MAS bills issuance on 6 November.

## What happened

### UK
* [HKMA, HSBC, Standard Chartered explore tokenised deposits](https://www.ledgerinsights.com/hkma-hsbc-stanchart-explore-tokenized-deposit-payments-via-swift-ledger-and-ensemble/) *(Ledger Insights)*: The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) is exploring connecting the Project Ensemble settlement layer to the Swift Ledger. HSBC, Standard Chartered, and Bank of China (Hong Kong) will participate. The initiative focuses on cross-border tokenised deposit payments in multiple currencies.
* [UK Treasury names banks for first digitally native gilt](https://www.leaprate.com/institutional/treasury-names-six-banks-to-lead-digit-the-uks-first-digitally-native-gilt/) *(LeapRate)*: HM Treasury appointed Barclays, HSBC, Lloyds, Morgan Stanley, NatWest, and RBC as joint lead managers for DIGIT. This is the UK's first digitally native gilt. The pilot is expected by the first quarter of 2027.
* [HSBC launches tokenised MMF in Hong Kong](https://www.ledgerinsights.com/hsbc-unveils-its-first-retail-tokenized-mmf-with-redcoin-as-onramp/) *(Ledger Insights)*: HSBC Asset Management in Hong Kong launched its first tokenised money market fund (MMF) on the Ethereum blockchain. It is accessible via the bank's mobile app with a minimum investment of HKD 100 using RedCoin.
* [Innovate Finance responds to UK stablecoin regulation](https://www.innovatefinance.com/consultation/bank-of-england-and-fca-approach-to-joint-regulation-of-systemic-stablecoin-issuers/): Innovate Finance and the Digital Pound Foundation have responded to the Bank of England and FCA's approach to jointly regulating systemic stablecoin issuers.

### Banks and payments
* [J.P. Morgan and Hyundai Card test AI agent payments](https://www.ledgerinsights.com/hyundai-card-jp-morgan-test-ai-agent-treasury-payments-on-dlt-standards-are-the-gap/) *(Ledger Insights)*: J.P. Morgan's Kinexys and Hyundai Card completed a proof of concept using an AI agent. The agent managed treasury liquidity in Hyundai Card's blockchain deposit account. It initiated FX conversions and required identity and mandate checks.
* [Twelve European banks choose Fireblocks for euro stablecoin](https://coinmarketcap.com/academy/article/12-european-banks-back-fireblocks-for-mica-compliant-euro-stablecoin) *(CoinMarketCap)*: A consortium of 12 European banks, operating as Qivalis, selected Fireblocks to build infrastructure for a MiCA-compliant euro stablecoin. Fireblocks will provide tokenisation technology, wallet infrastructure, and custody services. A launch is targeted for the second half of 2026.
* [DBS offers 24/7 USDC minting and burning](https://www.ledgerinsights.com/dbs-enables-24-7-minting-and-burning-of-usdc-stablecoin/) *(Ledger Insights)*: DBS announced an agreement with Circle enabling institutional clients to mint and burn USDC stablecoins 24/7. Eligible clients can access almost instant US dollar settlement.

### Funds, securities and market infrastructure
* [Hana Bank issues digital bond on Euroclear platform](https://coinmarketcap.com/academy/article/h-hana-bank-issues-dollar100m-digital-bond-on-euroclears-blockchain-platform) *(CoinMarketCap)*: Hana Bank issued a $100 million five-year digital bond using Euroclear's blockchain platform. This reduced settlement time to same-day from up to five business days.
* [State Street joins Equilend's 1Source DLT platform](https://www.ledgerinsights.com/state-street-joins-1source-equilends-dlt-platform/) *(Ledger Insights)*: State Street joined 1Source, Equilend's distributed ledger technology platform. The platform provides a golden source of truth for securities lending transactions, synchronising changes between institutions.

### Central banks
* [Bundesbank and MAS publish FX CBDC trial findings](https://www.ledgerinsights.com/bundesbank-mas-test-cross-border-fx-settlement-using-wholesale-cbdcs/) *(Ledger Insights)*: The Deutsche Bundesbank and Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) published key takeaways from Project Simbolon. This proof of concept tested FX PvP using wholesale CBDCs. Deutsche Bank and DBS participated, exchanging tokenised euros and Singapore dollars.

### Regulation and law
* [IOSCO publishes final report on asset tokenisation](https://www.regulationtomorrow.com/2026/11/iosco-final-report-on-the-tokenisation-of-financial-assets-2026/) *(Global Regulation Tomorrow)*: The International Organisation of Securities Commissions (IOSCO) published its Final Report on the Tokenisation of Financial Assets 2026 on 18 November. IOSCO observed that tokenisation has progressed but remains transitional and fragmented.
* [UK Treasury amends stablecoin rules](https://globaladvisoryexperts.com/news/uk-treasury-amends-cryptoasset-regime-stablecoin-rules) *(Global Advisory Experts)*: The UK Treasury amended its cryptoasset regime stablecoin rules via SI 2026/1219, made on 18 November. Most regulations came into force the day after being made. Regulations 2 and 3 will come into force on 25 October 2027.

## Worth watching
* [**ECB Tokenized Deposit and Securities Trials**](https://financefeeds.com/ecb-invites-financial-firms-to-test-tokenized-deposits-and-securities-under-appia/): The European Central Bank is inviting industry partners to apply for participation in its Appia tokenisation trials for deposits and securities, with applications due by 11 December.
* [**DTCC Solana Integration for Tokenized Equities**](https://www.coindesk.com/video/solana-foundations-nick-ducoff-on-dtcc-integration-and-the-tokenization-supercycle): The DTCC is planning an integration with the Solana blockchain by 2027 to expand its tokenisation services for equities.
